About

I'm a student at Arizona State University (formerly at Tulane University), studying business and building software alongside it. My focus is startup MVPs — proving an idea works with real users before spending months on architecture it doesn't need yet.

I'm an Eagle Scout (BSA Troop 200), which is where I first learned to see a big goal as a stack of small, finishable steps. I carry that into how I build: volunteer work with Vibha and FIRST Robotics, and with In the Wonder Code, a STEM non-profit introducing kids to programming.

On campus, I'm active in Alpha Kappa Psi (professional business fraternity) and Phi Delta Epsilon, where the throughline is the same one behind my ventures: business strategy and product design aren't separate disciplines — the best builders do both.
